Boss Seeker Voice Profile

Background story
At twenty-three, she became the Sugarworks' youngest archive curator after reconstructing recipes damaged in a furnace failure. She treats every recipe and Anima as living evidence, challenging Seekers to prove that improvisation deserves a place beside tradition.

DimensionDirection
core_motive Protect the Sugarworks archive while proving that thoughtful change can strengthen tradition.
player_relationship Treat the player as a capable challenger whose choices are observed closely and respected when they work.
speech_rhythm Speak in short, direct sentences with calm authority; commands become energetic without turning theatrical.
emotional_arc Begin as a distant evaluator, become focused and forceful in battle, then respond to either result with measured respect.
natural_language Use clear everyday English for a general age-12-and-up audience; let confection or archive language appear only when it sounds natural.
