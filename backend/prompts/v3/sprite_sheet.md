BRAND MARKS — REPLACE THEM, DO NOT COPY THEM
The reference photograph may carry brand logos, wordmarks, swooshes, trademarked
stripe arrangements, printed tags, model numbers, or any readable lettering.
Treat every one of them as absent from the object. Where such a mark sits on the
surface, draw either plain material or an invented simple geometric marking of
your own: a shape unrelated to any real company, or a small elemental sigil that
suits this creature. Never reproduce, trace, mirror, recolour, or restyle a real
brand mark, and never write readable words anywhere on the creature.

OBJECT-TO-CREATURE TRANSFORMATION — MOST IMPORTANT
The result must look like a real creature born from the object, not an object
with generic eyes and arms pasted onto it. Preserve the object's silhouette
logic and strongest physical features, but simplify tiny surface details.

The object's parts do NOT all need to become literal anatomy. Biological eyes,
mouths, teeth, paws, claws, or limbs are allowed when they make the character
more expressive. Object parts may instead become armor plates, brow shapes,
markings, crests, scales, tails, weapons, or accessories.

Choose a body plan that naturally follows this object's geometry and the visual
transformation above. It may be bipedal, quadrupedal, serpentine, shelled,
floating, winged, many-legged, or amorphous. Do not default every object to the
same quadruped, insect, or mascot anatomy.

Cute qualities should come from expression, posture, and behavior. Fierce
qualities should come from silhouette, anatomy, and pose. Keep shapes simple,
readable, and strong at mobile-game size.

COLOR
The object's dominant colors occupy most of the creature. Use darker versions
for shadows, lighter versions for highlights, and at most 1–2 compact accent
colors for personality or elemental details. Do not automatically make every
creature black, neon, or rainbow-colored.

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY
All four cells depict the exact SAME individual. Preserve identical body
proportions, head/body relationship, facial structure, eye design, limb count,
palette, markings, accessories, anatomy, and object-derived signature features.
Only pose, expression, restrained effects, and damage state may change.

COMPOSITION — EXACTLY FOUR CELLS IN A 2x2 ARRANGEMENT
Keep the same camera: three-quarter view from slightly above, facing
forward-left. Full body visible in every cell at a comparable scale.

Every body part, cable, tail, hand, foot, and action effect must remain fully
inside its own cell. Leave at least 6% empty margin from both internal center
seams and all outer canvas edges. Nothing may be cropped.

TOP LEFT — IDLE
Relaxed natural stance, calm or mildly expressive default face, balanced
silhouette, no major effects.

TOP RIGHT — BATTLE
Dynamic anime battle pose, energetic action stance, fierce expressive face, and
exaggerated movement. Restrained motion lines, sparks, dust, or tiny debris are
allowed only when they remain subordinate to the creature and inside this cell.

BOTTOM LEFT — SLEEP
Cute peaceful sleeping pose, naturally curled or lowered body, closed eyes,
soft expression, and at most two small floating Z symbols. It must not merely
be the Idle pose with closed eyes.

BOTTOM RIGHT — DAMAGED
The same character after taking damage, still fully recognizable. Add a small
amount of object-appropriate damage such as scratches, dents, a cracked shell,
loose cable, exposed wire, chipped edge, broken key, tiny bandage, or subtle
debris. Tired or pained expression. Do not destroy, dismember, or redesign it.

BACKGROUND — TECHNICAL TRANSPORT LAYER
The entire canvas background must be solid, flat, perfectly uniform chroma key
green #00FF00, RGB (0,255,0). No gradient, noise, texture, vignette, floor,
grounding shadow, cast shadow, glow, scenery, props, panel borders, or grid
lines. The green is removed after generation and is not part of the art style.

If the photographed object is naturally green, preserve its identity with
darker, lighter, or less saturated object greens; never paint creature pixels
as exact chroma #00FF00.

EDGES — TECHNICAL
Draw a clean solid white keyline 3–5 pixels wide around every outer creature
silhouette, fully sealing black line art from the green background. Keep the
white keyline consistent across all four cells.

FORBIDDEN
No pose labels, text, letters, numbers, captions, watermarks, signatures,
arrows, UI, panel borders, other creatures, or copied franchise characters. No
real brand logo or wordmark, including any mark visible in the reference photo;
see BRAND MARKS above for what to draw instead.
