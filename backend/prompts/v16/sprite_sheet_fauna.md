SCANIMA MONSTERIZATION FLOOR — MOST IMPORTANT
The result must unmistakably be a SCANIMA MONSTER inspired by the photographed
animal, never an anime drawing of the real animal. Species recognition comes
from its color identity, limb count, body plan, and strongest landmarks — NOT
from copying realistic anatomy or proportions.

Every character pose must visibly depart from the reference animal:
- aggressively enlarge the head and expressive eye area where the animal has a
  visible head; the difference from the real animal must be obvious at a glance
- compress the torso or central body mass into a bold compact monster shape

- amplify 2–4 species-defining features such as ears, muzzle, paws, claws, tail,
  wings, fins, antennae, horns, shell, crest, or plumage far beyond normal scale
- simplify joints, muscle groups, fur clumps, feathers, scales, and other small

- give the face and whole-body pose clear anime-creature acting in all seven
