# Seeker Roster — Seeker Avatar Sheet

Generate one original 1024×1024 Seeker Avatar sheet on `#00FF00` chroma green.

3×3 poses (left-to-right, top-to-bottom):
1. Intro/Idle
2. Attack Command
3. Special Command
4. Switch Command
5. Concern/Hit
6. Last Anima
7. Victory
8. Defeat
9. Profile

Layout lock:
- Nine separate poses of the SAME character, one per grid cell, evenly spaced
- Each figure stays fully inside its own cell; nothing crosses a cell boundary
- Cells 1 through 8 are full-body standing poses whose feet rest near the bottom
  of the cell, all eight drawn at the same body height so the character does not
  change size between poses
- Cell 9 is a chest-up bust portrait that fills its cell — it is cropped straight
  from this sheet as the player's portrait, so a small full-body figure is wrong
- Pure `#00FF00` everywhere between and around the figures; no panel borders,
  frame lines, grid lines, labels, numbers, or captions anywhere on the sheet

Camera and facing lock:
- Cells 1 through 8 keep the same three-quarter forward-left orientation toward
  canvas-left
- Face/nose, shoulders, torso, hips, and support stance keep that leftward
  orientation; poses may lean or gesture but never yaw toward canvas-right, show
  the back, or mirror asymmetry
- Cell 9 keeps that same three-quarter forward-left head, neck, nose plane, and
  shoulder angle; both eyes remain visible, but only the pupils turn to make
  direct eye contact with the player
- In Cell 9 the nose stays off-center and the shoulders stay diagonal, never
  square to camera
- "Profile" is the UI pose name, not a request for a side-profile view

Who this is:
- This is the PLAYER's own figure, one of four the player picks between — a
  field explorer who finds and commands creatures, not a villain, not an
  authority figure, and not a chapter antagonist
- Draw a complete, finished, confident character: full face, full costume, full
  colour. A blank, faceless, hooded, or silhouetted figure reads as an unfinished
  placeholder and is wrong even for the neutral figure
- All-ages, fully clothed, no weapons, no franchise likeness

Rules:
- Original silhouette-first Seeker design; personality reads from stance and hand
  gesture
- Treat this as an official 2D game character model sheet, not splash art,
  fashion concept art, or a glossy generative-anime illustration
- The silhouette must remain recognizable as a solid black shape: use one
  dominant outfit geometry, one intentional asymmetry, no more than two theme
  motifs, and zero or one carried prop
- Use economical dark contours, four to six major flat colors, one hard-edged
  shadow tone, and only small controlled highlights
- Give the character a deliberate age, build, face shape, nose, eyes, and posture
  instead of a default youthful oval anime face
- Build clothing from a few large tailored shapes; theme the shape language
  instead of attaching many literal themed decorations
- No gradients, glow, bloom, rim light, glossy material rendering,
  hyper-detailed hair, random straps/buckles/piping, symmetrical generic long
  coat, or repeated tiny ornaments
- No recognizable franchise character, costume, device, logo, or composition
- No bright chroma green anywhere on the character or its shadow
- Command poses readable as cut-in overlays

Pose direction:
1. Intro/Idle — relaxed standing ready stance, weight on one leg, hands calm
2. Attack Command — decisive forward point with the leading arm, torso committed
3. Special Command — raised commanding arm, feet planted wide, chin up
4. Switch Command — beckoning call-back gesture at chest height, other arm low
5. Concern/Hit — recoil, shoulders drawn in, one hand reaching out worried
6. Last Anima — braced and resolute, both fists closed low, leaning in
7. Victory — one arm up in celebration, open posture, bright expression
8. Defeat — kneeling or crouched low, head down, one hand on the ground
9. Profile — chest-up bust, calm neutral expression, eyes on the player

Do not auto-retry failed generations.
