Figure: the neutral Seeker — the roster default.

This figure is a real choice a player makes, not the game giving up on describing
them, so draw it with exactly as much face, costume, and colour as the other
three. It must never read as a silhouette, a mannequin, or a shape waiting for
art.

- Age and build: late twenties, average height, even medium build; shoulders and
  hips close to the same width
- Read: genuinely unplaceable as man or woman. No broad V-taper chest, no waist
  or bust emphasis, no facial hair, no makeup, no skirt, no earrings
- Face: soft square jaw, straight nose, level brows, calm steady eyes; a real
  finished face with a light easy expression
- Hair: chin-length blunt bob, tucked behind one ear, flat clean shapes
- Dominant outfit geometry: one straight-cut field wrap-coat closing edge-to-edge
  down the front over narrow trousers and low field boots
- Intentional asymmetry: the wrap's collar folds open over the left shoulder only
- Theme motifs (max two): a slim scanner cuff on the forearm, one flat lens disc
  clasp at the collar
- Palette: teal coat, deep slate-teal hard shadow, warm sand trim, one gold
  accent at the clasp
- Carried prop: none; both hands stay free
