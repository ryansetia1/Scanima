Figure: the automaton Seeker — a constructed Seeker, not a person in armour.

- Build: tall and lean, no age; plated limbs with visible simple joint rings at
  the shoulders, elbows, and knees
- Read: unmistakably a machine, but a warm companionable one — not a threatening
  war chassis, not a bulky mech, and not a human wearing a suit
- Head: smooth ovoid faceplate with one wide horizontal visor band as the only
  facial feature; the visor lens shifts shape a little per pose so the poses
  still read as feelings
- Antenna: one short antenna offset to one side, with a small disc at its tip
- Dominant outfit geometry: one long panelled mantle hanging from the shoulders
  over a plated torso and straight plated legs
- Intentional asymmetry: the mantle is cut away entirely over the right arm,
  leaving that arm's plating bare
- Theme motifs (max two): one flat lens disc set into the chest plate, banded
  joint rings
- Palette: pale slate plating, cool grey-blue hard shadow, off-white mantle, one
  warm orange accent in the visor band and chest lens
- Carried prop: none; both hands stay free
