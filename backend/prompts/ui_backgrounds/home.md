# Scanima — Home Sanctuary Background

Generate one original opaque 9:16 portrait PNG for the Home screen of a mobile
virtual-pet monster game. This is environment art only: no creature, person,
text, logo, watermark, button, card, HUD, or interface element.

Scene: a calm private Anima sanctuary at blue hour, combining a cozy companion
habitat with a restrained futuristic observation deck. A broad panoramic
opening looks toward a distant mysterious landscape and soft night sky. Use a
low circular companion platform and a few architectural ribs, habitat consoles,
or botanical silhouettes only at the outer left and right edges. The room
should feel safe, intimate, and inhabited, not sterile.

Composition for a tall phone:
- keep the central 58% of the frame open and low-detail from roughly 24% to 76%
  height; the player's Anima will stand there
- keep the upper 15% and lower 17% dark, quiet, and low contrast for persistent
  game chrome
- frame with natural asymmetry at the outer edges; no centered hero object
- do not draw a portal, magic circle, holographic rings, cage, throne, or UI-like
  reticle behind the creature

Visual lock: original polished 2D Japanese anime environment, clean confident
contours, flat base colors, crisp two-step cel shading, six to nine harmonized
color groups, selective material texture, large readable shapes, feature-
animation background quality. Palette: midnight indigo and deep navy, cool cyan
accents, restrained warm gold, muted violet. Warm and atmospheric but not
bright. No photorealism, PBR, glossy 3D, plastic toy rendering, bloom, lens
flare, volumetric fog, depth-of-field blur, painterly smearing, generic AI
detail soup, or recognizable franchise imagery.
