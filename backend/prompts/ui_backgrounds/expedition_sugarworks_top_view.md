# Scanima — The Sugarworks Top-View Expedition Map

Generate one original opaque 9:16 portrait PNG for the node-map screen of a
mobile game Expedition chapter named The Sugarworks. Show a high bird's-eye,
mostly top-down view over a long district of the Sugarworks realm, like viewing
a small confectionery country from above. This is illustrated world-map
environment art, not a combat arena and not literal board-game UI.

Build one continuous vertical journey:
- a broad, long central avenue runs from the bottom edge toward the top horizon;
  it occupies roughly the central 46–52% of the frame and is the calm open area
  where runtime route nodes will appear
- buildings, homes, workshops, gardens, tiny plazas, trees, canals, and factory
  infrastructure cluster only along the left and right sides
- the lower side districts suggest Gumdrop Yard through colorful masonry,
  rounded garden beds, compact homes, and playful but believable landscaping
- middle side districts suggest Caramel Foundry through copper roofs, functional
  pipes, vats, courtyards, and warm amber material accents
- upper side districts suggest Peppermint Furnace through ivory-and-raspberry
  striped chimneys, fortified workshops, and denser industrial silhouettes
- make the realm feel inhabited and traversable, with varied roof shapes and
  intentional districts, but never place people, vehicles, or creatures

Critical runtime-node composition:
- the central avenue must remain open, wide, matte, and low-detail for almost
  the full height; no buildings, trees, fountains, props, medians, crossroads,
  arches, bridges, gates, or decorative emblems inside it
- absolutely no route nodes, circles, connecting lines, dotted paths, arrows,
  labels, map pins, icons, signs, text, logos, watermark, buttons, or interface
  elements
- the avenue may use restrained large paving shapes and subtle wear, but no
  small repeating tiles or markings that could compete with node edges
- all rich detail belongs to the outer left and right 24–27%
- upper 16% and lower 17% stay darker and quieter for title, status, actions,
  and navigation

Theme lock: candy factory / confectionery expressed through functional
architecture, gardens, construction materials, and shape language—not a toy
diorama made from oversized literal candy. Use an original polished 2D Japanese
anime aerial environment, clean charcoal/plum contours, flat base colors, crisp
two-step cel shading, six to nine harmonized color groups, selective material
texture, and large readable forms. Palette: midnight navy and deep plum ground,
caramel copper, antique gold, dusty raspberry, muted mint, warm ivory, restrained
sky blue. No photorealism, PBR, glossy 3D, plastic toy rendering, bright white
paper, bloom, sparkle carpet, wallpaper patterns, micro-detail soup,
depth-of-field blur, painterly smearing, or recognizable franchise imagery.
