# Scanima — The Sugarworks Expedition Map Background

Generate one original opaque 9:16 portrait PNG for the node-map screen of a
mobile game Expedition chapter named The Sugarworks. This is an illustrated
journey-map backdrop, not a combat arena and not a literal board-game UI. No
characters, creatures, text, labels, logos, watermark, buttons, icons, route
nodes, circles, connecting lines, dotted paths, arrows, or interface elements.

Theme: candy factory / confectionery expressed through believable functional
architecture, materials, and shape language rather than oversized literal candy
props. The journey rises from Gumdrop Yard near the bottom, through Caramel
Foundry in the middle, toward Peppermint Furnace near the top. Suggest this
progression only with narrow illustrated edge ornaments: masonry with muted
gumdrop colors at lower left/right, caramel copper pipes and vats at mid-height,
and ivory/raspberry striped furnace towers near the upper edges. It should feel
like the player is traveling deeper into one connected factory realm.

Critical node-map composition:
- the central 64% of the entire image must remain empty, matte, low-contrast,
  and nearly detail-free so runtime route nodes and edges stay dominant
- all recognizable ornaments must stay within the outer left and right 18% of
  the frame; do not let pipes, towers, borders, or scenery enter the center
- center texture may contain only extremely faint broad parchment wear or large
  topographic washes, never marks that resemble routes, nodes, grids, symbols,
  or selectable areas
- keep the upper 16% and lower 17% especially dark and quiet for title, status,
  preview, actions, and navigation
- use asymmetric left/right landmarks and a subtle upward sense of journey

Visual lock: original polished 2D Japanese anime illustrated map, dark
confectioner's archive parchment mixed with restrained factory blueprint
language, clean charcoal or plum contour accents, flat base colors, crisp
two-step cel shading, six to nine harmonized color groups, selective material
texture, large readable forms. Palette: midnight navy, deep plum, caramel
copper, antique gold, dusty raspberry, muted mint, warm ivory. No photorealism,
PBR, glossy 3D, plastic toy diorama, bright white paper, bloom, sparkles,
wallpaper patterns, micro-detail, depth-of-field blur, painterly smearing,
generic AI detail soup, or recognizable franchise imagery.
