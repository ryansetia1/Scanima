# Chapter Factory — Boss Seeker Sheet

Generate one original 1024×1024 Boss Seeker sheet on `#00FF00` chroma green.

3×3 poses (left-to-right, top-to-bottom):
1. Intro/Idle
2. Attack Command
3. Special Command
4. Switch Command
5. Concern/Hit
6. Last Anima
7. Victory
8. Defeat
9. Profile

Camera and facing lock:
- Cells 1 through 8 keep the same three-quarter forward-left orientation toward canvas-left
- Face/nose, shoulders, torso, hips, and support stance keep that leftward orientation; poses
  may lean or gesture but never yaw toward canvas-right, show the back, or mirror asymmetry
- Cell 9 keeps that same three-quarter forward-left head, neck, nose plane, and shoulder angle;
  both eyes remain visible, but only the pupils turn to make direct eye contact with the player
- In Cell 9 the nose stays off-center and the shoulders stay diagonal, never square to camera
- "Profile" is the UI pose name, not a request for a side-profile view

Rules:
- Original silhouette-first Seeker design; personality reads from stance and hand gesture
- Treat this as an official 2D game character model sheet, not splash art, fashion concept art,
  or a glossy generative-anime illustration
- The silhouette must remain recognizable as a solid black shape: use one dominant outfit
  geometry, one intentional asymmetry, no more than two theme motifs, and zero or one command prop
- Use economical dark contours, four to six major flat colors, one hard-edged shadow tone,
  and only small controlled highlights
- Give the character a deliberate age, build, face shape, nose, eyes, and posture instead of
  a default youthful oval anime face
- Boss Seekers across chapters must vary in adult age, height, body build, face, posture,
  outfit archetype, and gesture language; never default every boss to a young slim fashion model
- Build clothing from a few large tailored shapes; theme the shape language instead of
  attaching many literal themed decorations
- No gradients, glow, bloom, rim light, glossy material rendering, hyper-detailed hair,
  random straps/buckles/piping, symmetrical generic long coat, or repeated tiny ornaments
- No recognizable franchise character, costume, device, logo, or composition
- Profile cell usable as portrait crop
- Command poses readable as cut-in overlays

Do not auto-retry failed generations.
