# Chapter Factory — Zone Background Art

Generate one opaque, crisp high-resolution 16:9 landscape PNG for an Expedition
combat arena. Use the largest exact 16:9 output available (target 1536×864 or
larger). This image is a wide establishing-shot Battle backdrop, not a node-map
illustration or a close camera view.

Style lock — match the chapter's Anima and Boss Seeker:
- original hand-painted 2D anime environment with believable space and
  functional architecture; polished feature-animation background quality
- large readable shapes first, then a restrained second tier of detail:
  six to nine harmonized color groups, clean charcoal or plum line accents,
  two controlled shadow/value steps, and selective material texture
- natural asymmetry, subtle wear, varied edges, and a few small structural
  details may enrich the middle distance; never repeat detail across the frame
- keep the central combat sightline open so fighters stay the focus after
  mobile downscale
- integrate the confection theme into materials, construction, and shape
  language; do not turn the environment into a toy diorama made from oversized
  literal candy props
- crisp controlled contour edges at native resolution; no pixelation, soft
  focus, depth-of-field blur, painterly smearing, or fake upscale artifacts

Combat floor — required:
- only the lower 22–26% is one continuous solid floor plane (tile, slab,
  packed court, or platform) wide enough for two fighters and a Boss Seeker
- the bottom 14–18% must stay unbroken across the center 60% of the width
- no liquid, lava, syrup, rails, gutters, chasms, vents, fences, or props
  under the fighter foot line
- no oversized foreground floor slabs or low-angle perspective that makes the
  ground dominate the image
- spectacle (vats, silos, furnaces, gates) stays in the middle-distance band,
  behind a clear floor edge or low wall

Composition:
- follow the supplied Zone composition direction; vary the spatial grammar
  across zones so the chapter does not reveal a repeated layout template
- do not default to a centered hero object. A centered composition is allowed
  occasionally when the Zone direction asks for it, but build it from open
  depth and unequal framing forms rather than one object on a focal pedestal
- compose for tall-phone side cropping without placing essential landmarks at
  either extreme edge
- distant eye-level camera with a broad field of view, not a close-up
- reserve roughly the upper 40–45% for open readable sky with only restrained
  cloud shapes; do not fill it with towers, pipes, roofs, or overhangs
- place the main architecture across the middle 30–35% so the world remains
  identifiable without consuming the sky or combat floor
- leave the right-third lower band uncluttered for the Boss Seeker silhouette
- no characters, creatures, text, logos, sign lettering, watermark, UI, or
  recognizable IP landmarks

Forbidden: photoreal or PBR rendering, glossy 3D, plastic toy rendering,
airbrush gradients, bloom, glow, steam/particle haze, crystalline sparkle
carpets, perfectly symmetric layouts, and AI-detail soup.

Do not auto-retry failed generations.
