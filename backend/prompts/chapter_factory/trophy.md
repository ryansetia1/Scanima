# Chapter Factory — Chapter Core Trophy

Generate one square Chapter-specific **Inner Core** PNG on a flat `#00FF00` chroma green
backdrop. The canonical simple point-top Hexagonal Vessel is composited afterward; do not
draw it.

Locked two-layer Chapter Core v3 grammar:
- Generate only one centered, front-facing Inner Core that fits safely inside the Vessel's
  central hexagonal window
- Keep scale, visual weight, dark contour thickness, flat rendering, and complexity consistent
  across chapters, but do not reuse an identical outer contour
- The chapter-specific silhouette may be rounded, angular, faceted, folded, asymmetric, or
  otherwise distinctive; shape it through a few large pinches, cuts, lobes, points, or facet
  groups and keep it recognizable when filled solid black
- Theme must shape both the outer silhouette and internal construction; never stamp a random
  emblem or letter onto the center of a generic gem
- Use six to ten large color regions maximum, four or five flat colors, one hard shadow plane,
  and one small highlight plane
- A thematic object may inspire the shape language, but flatten it into an original Core
  construction rather than rendering a literal prop or miniature scene
- Leave generous green space around the Inner Core so it can be scaled inside the Vessel
- No glass shell, orb, crystal container, outer frame, pedestal, floor shadow, aura, glow,
  particles, realistic transparency, text, logo, badge, medal, coin, or recognizable IP symbol
- Dark contour touches green directly; no white matte, checkerboard, or bright chroma-green facet
- Readable at small profile thumbnail size

Do not auto-retry failed generations.
