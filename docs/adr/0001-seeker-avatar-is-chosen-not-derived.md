# Seeker Avatar is chosen, not derived from Seeker Demographics

Onboarding already collects an optional gender answer, so the cheapest way to
give a Seeker a face would be to map that answer onto avatar art. We rejected
that: two of its values (`another_identity`, `prefer_not_to_say`) have no honest
visual mapping, and deriving an appearance from a question the player answered
about themselves — a question that deliberately offers a way not to answer —
forces a look onto exactly the players who declined to be described. The Seeker
Avatar is therefore its own choice, made from a fixed roster, and Seeker
Demographics never feed art.

## Consequences

The roster must contain at least one figure that reads as non-gendered, so that
"I would rather not be depicted as either" is a real choice a player makes
instead of a fallback the game picks for them. Avatar choice must also be
changeable on its own, without touching demographics.
